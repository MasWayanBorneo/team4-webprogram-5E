Tour

International Tour Begins

June 1, 2026 • 4 min read

Following the sold-out North American run, Why Don't We has confirmed an international leg that will take them to Europe, Asia, and Latin America for the first time since 2019. The global extension of the Unfinished Business Tour runs from December 2026 through March 2027.

European Dates

The European leg opens on December 5 in London at the O2 Arena, followed by dates in Paris, Amsterdam, Berlin, Madrid, and Stockholm. The band will also make their first-ever appearance in Dublin and Warsaw before wrapping Europe on December 30 in Milan.

"International fans have been waiting a long time and so have we. We cannot wait to finally be in the same room as you." — Jack Avery

Asia-Pacific

January brings the band to the Asia-Pacific region, with confirmed shows in Tokyo, Seoul, Manila, Jakarta, Singapore, and Sydney. The Southeast Asia leg alone spans 8 cities across 3 weeks — the most comprehensive run the band has ever done in the region.

Latin America

The tour closes out in Latin America through February and March, hitting São Paulo, Buenos Aires, Bogotá, Mexico City, and Santiago. Fan-club pre-sale for all international dates opens June 10. General on-sale follows on June 14.

Full international dates are listed on the Tour page. This one is going to be special — do not miss your city.
