Announcement

Tour Dates Revealed

May 15, 2026 • 3 min read

The wait is over. Why Don't We has officially announced their 2026 North American headline tour — 32 cities, three months, and what promises to be the biggest production the band has ever brought to the road.

Full Tour Routing

The Unfinished Business Tour kicks off on September 5 in Seattle, WA and wraps on November 28 in Miami, FL. Major stops include New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Dallas, and Toronto.

"We have been dying to get back out there. This tour is going to feel like one long reunion with everyone who has stuck with us." — Jack Avery

Tickets

Fan club pre-sale opens May 20 at 10 AM local time. General on-sale follows on May 22 via all major ticketing platforms. VIP packages — including soundcheck access, a meet-and-greet, and exclusive merchandise — are available in limited quantities.

Support Acts

Support acts will be announced on a city-by-city basis over the coming weeks. Follow the band's Instagram and Twitter for the latest updates as each date is confirmed.

Full dates and venue listings are available on the Tour page. Don't sleep on tickets — several markets sold out within hours during the last run.
